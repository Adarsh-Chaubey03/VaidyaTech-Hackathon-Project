## 🏆 Project Overview  

**VaidyaTek** is an **AI-powered healthcare platform** developed as a **Hackathon Project**.  
It bridges patients, doctors, labs, and pharmacies into one seamless ecosystem — featuring a unique **AI Skin Disease Detection System**.  

> “Your Health, Our Innovation” — delivering smart, patient-centric healthcare with AI and real-time insights.

---

## 📋 Summary  

VaidyaTek redefines digital healthcare by offering:  
- AI-powered diagnosis and skin analysis  
- Smart appointment booking (online/offline)  
- Video consultations  
- Lab test booking & medicine purchase  
- Awareness dashboard for outbreaks  
- Secure wallet and transparent refunds  

Together, it makes healthcare **faster, smarter, and more accessible**.

## 🩺 Problem at a Glance  

| ❌ Challenge | 💡 Description |
|--------------|----------------|
| Long wait times | Patients face delays due to poor scheduling. |
| Specialist availability | Hard to find trusted doctors, labs, or pharmacies nearby. |
| Payments & refunds | No standardized or transparent system. |
| Awareness gap | Lack of updates on real-time disease outbreaks. |
| Preventive care | Expensive and hard to access for common users. |

---

## 🚀 Our Solution  

A unified **digital healthcare ecosystem** that empowers every stakeholder:  

✅ **AI Diagnosis & Skin Disease Detection** – Instant ML-based health checks using EfficientNet-B1 and OpenCV.  
✅ **Smart Appointments** – Book online/offline visits and video consultations in real time.  
✅ **Lab & Pharmacy Access** – Book lab tests, upload reports, and purchase medicines.  
✅ **Awareness Dashboard** – Stay updated with live viral alerts and prevention tips.  
✅ **Secure Wallet & Refunds** – Razorpay integration for safe transactions and instant refunds.  
✅ **Verified Doctor Onboarding** – Only authenticated and trusted professionals onboarded.
